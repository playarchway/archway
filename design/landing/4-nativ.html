Set up while you read the intro.

The first time you open Archway, it gets everything ready on its own. You don’t pick Wine versions, graphics backends or Windows DLLs.

  • Rosetta is available
  • Runtime downloaded and verified, 145 MB
  • Windows environment created
  • Installing Windows Steam

Steam stays in the background.

Archway hands your downloads to Steam and runs it silently. Its window appears once, so you can sign in on Steam’s own page. Archway never sees your password.

Leaves nothing behind.

Settings show exactly how much space Archway uses. Remove Archway deletes its data, Windows environment and games, then moves the app to the Trash.
