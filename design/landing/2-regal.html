Every game wears a label.

Archway is early. It tells you what it knows instead of promising that everything runs.

Tested

Starts with a known setup and no manual steps. Today that is Brick by Brick: Tidy Up the Small Town!

Untested

Try any other game you own. It may run, it may not; Archway won’t pretend to know.

Out of reach

Games with kernel anti-cheat, and most big AAA titles. Archway doesn’t aim for those.

Ready when you are.

On first start Archway downloads its runtime and Windows Steam. You sign in once on Steam’s own page; Archway never sees your password.
